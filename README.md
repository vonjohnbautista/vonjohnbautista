I'm a developer who enjoys turning business requirements into useful applications, automations, and integrations.

My work brings together **Zoho applications, Deluge scripting, and web development**. I enjoy connecting systems, building custom interfaces, and making everyday workflows easier to manage.

- 🛠️ **My focus:** Zoho customisation, business automation, and application development.
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Zoho applications, Deluge, React, and Angular.
- 🌱 **Always learning:** Exploring languages, tools, and better ways to build.
- 🤝 **Let's talk:** CRM workflows, custom widgets, and integration challenges.
- 📫 **Reach me:** [von@thecrmcarpenters.com](mailto:von@thecrmcarpenters.com)

## How I approach development

- **Understand the workflow.** Get clear on the problem before writing code.
- **Keep it readable.** Choose clear names and straightforward implementation.
- **Build reusable foundations.** Turn useful patterns into templates and components.
- **Document the essentials.** Make setup and handover easier for the next developer.
- **Keep improving.** Learn from feedback, experiments, and the people using the software.
